# Malsburg 1637

Author: Maxim Egorov

An AI-assisted decipherment proposal for a separate ten-line alphabetic block in the 1637 correspondence, covering 510 retained normalized letters with a period-five transformation. The article preserves its transcription cautions, literal defects, unresolved markers and revised English interpretation.

- [Complete published article](article.md)

- [Sources and earlier work](sources.md)

Daniel Bourdeau’s foundational work and the later contribution credited to Larry Beck with ChatGPT are distinguished in the article. The source is digital image 0012; an unverified manuscript folio number is not asserted. External historical and palaeographic verification remains pending.
